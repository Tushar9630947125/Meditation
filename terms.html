Terms & Policies

01

Terms & Conditions

  • The spiritual healing, and meditation sessions provided by Blessing Welfare Society and Manisha Yadav are intended for personal growth, spiritual awareness, and relaxation.
  • These services are not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or psychiatric evaluation and treatment. Always consult a healthcare provider for any health-related issues.
  • We reserve the right to terminate a session immediately if a participant is abusive, disruptive, or under the influence of illicit substances.
02

Privacy Policy

  • We respect your privacy. Any personal information (such as name, email, phone number, and personal struggles shared during sessions) is strictly confidential.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties under any circumstances.
  • Data collected through our contact and booking forms is solely used to communicate with you regarding your scheduled sessions.
03

Appointment Policy

  • All appointments must be booked in advance via our website portal or WhatsApp.
  • If you need to cancel or reschedule, please notify us at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled time.
  • Missed appointments without prior notice may not be eligible for a refund or rescheduling.
04

Refund Policy

  • BLESSING WELFARE SOCIETY believes in helping its customers as far as possible, and has therefore a liberal cancellation policy.
  • Under this policy: Cancellations will be considered only if the request is made immediately after registering for a workshop.
  • If you need to cancel your registration, you can do so up to 24 hours after registering and receive a full refund. This allows us sufficient time to fill your spot and manage our workshop effectively.
  • If you cancel after 24 hours of registering for a workshop, you will not be eligible for a refund. However, we offer the option to apply the fee towards a future workshop within the next 2 months.
