Our
Vision &
Values

At Blessing Welfare Society, our mission is to make spirituality simple, practical, and accessible for everyone. We believe in empowering individuals to live a balanced and meaningful life by connecting with their inner selves.

Our guiding values are:

  • •
    Authenticity: We bring real, time-tested spiritual practices that lead to true transformation.
  • •
    Balance: We focus on harmonizing the mind, body, and spirit to help you achieve inner peace.
  • •
    Compassion: We nurture a supportive and inclusive community where everyone feels valued and understood.

By integrating ancient wisdom into modern living, we inspire self-discovery, healing, and purposeful living. At Blessing Welfare Society, we're here to guide you toward a life of clarity, joy, and inner alignment.

About Me.

Manisha Yadav is the founder of Blessing Welfare Society. She is a meditation maven and spiritual mentor who helps people find peace, clarity, and purpose through ancient wisdom and modern practices from past 10 Years.

Manisha Yadav

Her journey started with her own search for inner peace. Facing life's struggles, Manisha discovered meditation as a powerful tool for healing and transformation. This experience inspired her to share this knowledge with others.

Blessing Welfare Society, her platform, is built on three core values:

  • Authenticity: Sharing genuine, time-tested practices.
  • Simplicity: Making ancient teachings easy to understand.
  • Transformation: Helping people unlock their full potential.

At Blessing Welfare Society, Manisha blends ancient teachings with practical tools for today's fast-paced world. Her relatable and easy-to-follow methods have inspired many to embrace a life of mindfulness and spiritual growth.
